Space.

You open your eyes to a blindingly white room of sorts. You can't see any walls — or a floor, or ceiling, for that matter — but you can barely make out a figure standing in front of you. You feel as though it can understand you.

> ...Where am I?

A figure stands in front of you. You are unable to make out much of what this being is, but notice beautiful wings sprouting from its back.
You made it!
A voice resounds from the inside of your head. It is not a language you know, but you understand nevertheless.

> What are you?

You try to take a closer look, but to no avail; it's too bright to make out any features.
I am Kalliopeia, it says. Architect and master of this domain.

> Where do I go now?

A doorway appears to your side.
Feel free to explore where your heart desires.The being smiles at you. It disappears as you blink.
